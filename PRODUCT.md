# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Automotive exhibitors, brand sponsors, and marketing decision-makers in Saudi Arabia and the wider Gulf region deciding whether to book a stand at JIMS 2026. Secondary audience: press, VIP guests, and the general public researching the show (dates, location, what to expect). Context: evaluating a six-figure-SAR booking decision, often on mobile, comparing JIMS against other regional motor shows.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for the Jeddah International Motor Show (JIMS) 2026 — the 20th edition, organized by Integrated Solutions Co. for Events under JCEE (Jeddah Center for Exhibitions and Events). Converts exhibitor leads (booking a Custom Stand, Plug & Play Booth, or Thematic Space) and informs press/VIP/public attendees of the Press Day, VIP Night, and Visitor Days programme. Success = exhibitor enquiries and registration-form submissions.

## Brand Personality

Premium, minimal, confident. Quieter luxury register: restrained red (#ff0f10) on near-black, sharp unrounded geometry, generous negative space. Motion and ornament stay deliberate and sparing rather than loud or maximalist — the brand earns attention through precision, not noise.

## Anti-references

Must not read as a generic SaaS template: no hero-metric-card clichés, no gradient-text, no identical bento-grid feature cards, no glassmorphism-as-decoration. Avoid the inverse failure too (generic used-car-dealer / rental-site look) — this is a premium automotive exhibition, not a listings marketplace.

## Design Principles

1. **Precision over noise** — every red accent, every piece of motion is deliberate; restraint signals premium confidence more than density does.
2. **Source-truthful content** — copy and figures come from the Exhibitor Booklet verbatim or a faithful paraphrase; never invent stats or quotes.
3. **Photography carries the brand** — the booklet's own renders/photography do the emotional work; UI chrome (cards, borders) stays quiet underneath them.
4. **Sharp, not soft** — no rounded corners, matching the extracted Framer design tokens (Bebas Neue display type, Geist Mono uppercase body, zero border-radius).
5. **Mobile-first conversion path** — exhibitor enquiry and registration CTAs must stay reachable one thumb-reach away on mobile, where much of the Gulf audience will browse.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA (contrast, keyboard nav, prefers-reduced-motion support). Noted future requirement, not yet in scope: bilingual Arabic/English with RTL layout support for the Arabic variant — flag before considering the site launch-complete for a Saudi audience.
